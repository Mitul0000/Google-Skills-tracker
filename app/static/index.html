How This Tool Works

Designed for Google Developer Group (GDG) organizers to verify badge completions for the Learn With Communities campaign.

1. Upload Profile CSV
Upload a CSV containing participants' public Google Skills profile URLs. The system normalizes URLs, preserves original row order, and filters out duplicates for network efficiency.
2. Live Verification Engine
The scraper inspects public profile markup, verifying each earned badge against required campaign badges using strict title normalization, date checking, and profile ownership validation.
3. Real-Time Streaming & Reporting
Progress streams live via Server-Sent Events (SSE). Results are categorized into score buckets (10/10 down to 0/10), with downloadable reports in CSV and JSON formats.

Key Features

Polite Rate Limiting & AIMD
Built-in token bucket rate limiting (8 req/s) with concurrency caps, exponential backoff, and adaptive slow-down to respect platform bandwidth.
Intelligent Caching
Completed 10/10 profiles are cached to avoid redundant fetches on re-uploads, while partial or private profiles are automatically re-checked.
Privacy & Injection Protection
No permanent accounts or tracking. Ephemeral SQLite storage expires after 2 hours of inactivity, and CSV export defends against formula injection attacks.
