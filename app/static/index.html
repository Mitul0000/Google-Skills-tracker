How This Tool Works

Designed for Google Developer Group (GDG) organizers to verify badge completions for the Learn With Communities campaign.

Step 1
Upload Profile CSV
Upload your CSV list with public Google Skills profile URLs. The tracker automatically normalizes URLs, preserves original input order, and de-duplicates requests to optimize network usage.
Step 2
Live Verification Engine
The scanner fetches public profile markup, matching earned badges against the 10 required campaign badges using strict title normalization, completion date verification, and ownership checks.
Step 3
Real-Time Streaming & Reporting
Track live progress with Server-Sent Events (SSE). Results are organized into score tiers (10/10 target down to 0/10) and available for immediate export as CSV and JSON reports.

Key Features

Engineered for speed, accuracy, and platform etiquette.

Reliability
Polite Rate Limiting & AIMD
Built-in token bucket rate limiting (8 req/s) with concurrency controls, exponential backoff, and adaptive AIMD slow-down to strictly respect Google Skills platform limits.
Performance
Intelligent Caching
Verified 10/10 profiles are cached to eliminate redundant re-scans across uploads, while incomplete or private profiles are automatically re-evaluated.
Security
Privacy & Injection Protection
Zero permanent tracking. Ephemeral SQLite storage auto-expires after 2 hours of inactivity, and CSV downloads feature built-in formula injection defenses.
